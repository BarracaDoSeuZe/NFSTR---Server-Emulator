Copy/extract a portable MariaDB distribution into this folder.

Required layout:
  mariadb\bin\mariadbd.exe
  mariadb\bin\mariadb.exe
  mariadb\bin\mariadb-install-db.exe
  mariadb\share\...

MariaDB binaries are intentionally NOT included in this package.
The first run creates mariadb\data locally.

Default DB port for this NFS package: 1979
